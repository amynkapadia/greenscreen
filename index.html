GreenScreen

A personal desktop tool for publishing to the owner's own YouTube channels.

GreenScreen runs on its owner's Mac. It prepares videos for the owner's own YouTube channels and, only after the owner approves each one, uploads it through the YouTube Data API. It also reads those channels' YouTube Analytics so the owner can see how videos perform.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it is not offered to other users.

Google access it asks for

  • Upload videos to and manage the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Read the channel's details and its YouTube Analytics reports, including revenue reports.

See the privacy policy for how this data is handled.
