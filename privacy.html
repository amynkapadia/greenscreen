Greenroom privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Greenroom is a personal desktop application used only by its owner to publish videos to, and read analytics for, the owner's own YouTube channels. Greenroom uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Greenroom receives an OAuth access token and refresh token from Google. With them it reads the channel's id and title, uploads videos the owner has approved, sets their privacy and schedule, and reads the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and estimated revenue).

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in a local database, and analytics are stored in the same local database. Greenroom has no server, and it does not send this data to any third party. It does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until the channel is disconnected or the access is revoked. Analytics are kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through Google's security settings page. After revocation Greenroom can no longer reach the channel, and the stored tokens stop working.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/amynkapadia/greenscreen/issues.
